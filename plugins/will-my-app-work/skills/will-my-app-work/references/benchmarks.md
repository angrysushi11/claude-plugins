# Benchmarks

Checked: 26 October 2026. Every number below comes from a public source named beside it, with its year and link. When web search is available, look for a newer edition of the same report first and prefer its numbers; say which edition you used. If a number you need isn't here, ask the founder for theirs or reason without it. Never fill a gap from memory.

How to read them:

- **Median** is where a typical app lands. **Top quartile (TQ)** is the line the best 25% of apps clear. A product usually needs at least 2–3 of its deciding numbers in the top quartile to work.
- Compare like with like: the same definition, time window and market. A blended global number hides big regional gaps: e-commerce installs cost $0.98 on average worldwide but $2.70 in North America (section 2).
- A dash means the source gives no value for that cell.

## 1. Subscription apps

Source: RevenueCat, *State of Subscription Apps 2026* (published March 2026; 2025 data; 115,000+ apps, $16B+ revenue; iOS, Android and web). https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps

The report's definitions:

- **Download-to-trial:** share of installs that start a free trial within 30 days of download.
- **Download-to-paid (D35):** share of installs that make at least one paid subscription purchase within 35 days of install.
- **Trial-to-paid:** share of free trial starts that convert into a paid subscription.
- **Revenue per install (RPI):** total revenue divided by total installs, at day 14 or day 60.
- **Realized LTV (RLTV) per payer:** the net value of an average paying user over a period (month 1 or year 1). The report doesn't say whether store fees are deducted.
- **Price tier:** how an app's average price compares with other apps (below average, average, above average). The report gives no price ranges for the tiers.
- **Hard paywall** means users pay (or start a trial) before using the app; **freemium** means a free tier with paid upgrades.

### What a new app usually earns

Same report as above.

- Monthly revenue one year after launch, all categories: median about $72; top quartile above $429; top 10% above $2,574.
- Within two years of launch, 17.3% of new apps reach $1K in monthly revenue and 4.6% reach $10K.
- Apps that reach $1K monthly revenue take a median of 58 days; $10K, 109 days.
- Apps launched before 2020 earn 69% of all subscription revenue: in most categories the incumbents already hold the money.

### Renewals and retention of payers

Source: RevenueCat, *State of Subscription Apps 2026*, retention section (year-1 retention compares 2023 and 2024 cohorts). https://www.revenuecat.com/sosa-2026-insights-part-2

- First annual renewal: median 23–40% depending on category (Productivity lowest at 23%).
- First renewal of weekly and monthly plans: 53–61%.
- Still paying one year after subscribing (2024 cohorts): annual plans 28%, monthly plans 8%, weekly plans 1.2%.
- 95% of annual subscribers who cancel never come back.

Older, but the only source here with a top quartile for renewals: RevenueCat, *Annual vs monthly renewal rates* (2022, updated June 2024; 10,000+ apps). ## 2. Cost of an install

Cost per install depends more on the market, the channel and the event a campaign optimizes for than on the category. Treat these as reference points; the founder's own test beats all of them.

**Apple Ads, search results.** Source: MobileAction, *Apple Ads 2026 Benchmark Report* (2025 data from MobileAction and SearchAds.com accounts). https://www.mobileaction.co/report/apple-ads-2026-benchmark-report/executive-summary/

- Average cost per install (CPA): $2.51 in 2025, down from $2.76 in 2024.
- By country, 2025: United Kingdom $4.53, United States $3.85, Australia $3.02, Japan $2.89, Canada $2.81, France $1.94, Italy $1.60; average across countries $2.83. https://www.mobileaction.co/report/apple-ads-2026-benchmark-report/search-results-ads-regional-cpa/
- Tap-through rate 8.08%; taps that became installs 65.82%.
- Cost per tap was lowest in Q2 ($1.26) and highest in Q4 ($1.88).

Apple defines average CPA as campaign spend divided by installs. These ads reach people already searching the App Store: one channel, not the market price of every install.

**Adjust** (global averages across apps measured by Adjust):

- E-commerce apps: $0.98 in 2025, down from $1.00. Games: $0.56 in 2025, up 30%. Source: Adjust, *Mobile App Trends 2026* (February 2026). https://www.adjust.com/resources/ebooks/mobile-app-trends-2026/
- E-commerce apps by region: North America $2.70, Europe $1.66, Asia-Pacific $0.90. Source: Adjust, *Shopping app trends* (October 2025). https://www.adjust.com/blog/shopping-app-trends/
- Finance apps: $1.13, down from $1.51. Source: Adjust, *Finance app insights 2025* (October 2025). https://www.adjust.com/blog/finance-app-insights-2025/

## 3. Retention for apps without subscriptions

- **Mobile games.** Source: GameAnalytics, *2026 Mobile & PC Gaming Benchmarks* (2025 data, mobile games with at least 1,000 monthly active users). Day 1: median about 22% at the start of 2025, falling through the year; top quartile just above 30%. Day 7: median just under 4%; top quartile 6–7%. Day 30: median about 0.7–0.8%; top quartile 1.6–1.8%. https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-mobile-pc-gaming-benchmarks
- **Shopping apps.** Day-1 retention: e-commerce average 13%, marketplace apps 24.8%. Source: Adjust, *Shopping app trends* (October 2025). https://www.adjust.com/blog/shopping-app-trends/

There is no sourced number here for ad revenue per user. For an ad-funded app, ask the founder for theirs or reason without it.

## 4. Numbers for a first test

- **Landing pages:** median conversion rate across industries 6.6%. Source: Unbounce, *Conversion Benchmark Report* (2024 edition; 41,000+ landing pages, 57M+ conversions; counts clicks and form fills). https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/
- **Meta (Facebook) ads, all industries, averages:** traffic campaigns 1.93% click-through rate and $0.60 per click; lead campaigns 2.70% click-through, $1.80 per click, 8.54% conversion rate, $27.39 per lead. Source: LocaliQ, *Facebook Ads Benchmarks* (updated September 2026; LocaliQ advertisers in 20 industries, few of them apps). https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/

Sizing a test: at $0.60 per click, 300–500 clicks cost about $180–300. A few hundred clicks is enough to tell a 2% sign-up rate from a 10% one, not 5% from 6%.

## 5. Store fees: what is left of the price

- **Apple App Store:** standard commission 30% (Apple press release, November 2020, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201118005376/en); 15% for developers with up to $1M in proceeds in the previous calendar year, and for new developers, under the App Store Small Business Program. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/
- **Google Play:** auto-renewing subscriptions 15%; in the US, UK, EEA, Australia and Japan this is shown as a 10% service fee plus a 5% billing fee. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622

Web payments skip the store commission but pay a payment processor; use the processor's current price.

## Worked check: does paid acquisition pay back?

Revenue per install ≈ download-to-paid × value per payer. Compare it with what an install costs in the founder's main market and channel.

North American medians: 2.6–2.8% download-to-paid × $26.07 year-1 value per payer ≈ $0.68–0.73 per install in the first year. A US Apple Ads search install costs $3.85, about five times more. With both numbers in the top quartile (6.0% × $46 ≈ $2.76), a first-year install still earns less than it costs, so a third lever is needed: a higher price, a hard paywall, cheaper installs from an organic channel, or a longer payback window the founder can fund. The figures come from different sources and samples, so treat the result as an order of magnitude, not a forecast.
